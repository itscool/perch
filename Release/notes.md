# Perch 2.0.301 — What’s new

- **Copy on one Mac, paste on another.** Text you copy is on every Mac of the desk straight away. For pictures, longer text, files and folders, press ⌃⌥⌘V on the Mac you are working on to bring the copy over, with a progress bar that closes itself. Passwords and other private copies never leave the Mac.
- **A newer Mac can update the others.** When another Mac on the desk runs an older Perch, choose Update Perch on that Mac from the menu, and it updates itself without anyone clicking there.
- **The menu bar shows your desk.** Two birds when your Macs are connected, a heart for each one sharing, and an envelope when a copy is waiting.
- **Disk space in the System section:** what macOS and your data take on the startup disk, what is free, and each other disk.
- **The Agent Kill Switch is gone.** Perch no longer watches every process on your Mac, so it is lighter. If Setup shows Old process watcher, press Remove… to take the last piece off.
- **A Mac on a different version of Perch is told to update,** instead of seeing "connection reset".

Every Mac on the desk needs this version.

Perch requires Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. See Settings → Updates → Release history for the complete history of Perch releases.
