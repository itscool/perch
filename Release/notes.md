# Perch 2.0.205 — What’s new

- **Desk pointer:** when control moves to another Mac, the pointer no longer stays behind on the Mac you left, and it carries on from where it was instead of jumping, including when you pick up the other Mac’s mouse. Update every Mac on your desk to get the full fix.
- **Desk sharing:** Perch only takes over the keyboard and mouse when your screens sit next to each other in Desk. If they don’t, each Mac keeps its own pointer and Desk tells you why.
- **Permissions:** when Perch’s signature changes, it removes the old Accessibility and Input Monitoring entries that no longer work, so you approve Perch once without a lookalike entry left behind.
- **Prevent idle lock:** now says how long your Mac stays unlocked.

Perch requires Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. See Settings → Updates → Release history for the complete history of Perch releases.
